# More achievements, safer links, and what's worth adding next

## 1. A much bigger achievement set

Today there are 10 achievements from 7 metrics. Expand to ~40 across five categories, each with a rarity tier, XP and coin reward on unlock, and an unlock toast.

**Categories**

- Consistency: 3 / 7 / 14 / 30 / 60 / 100 day streaks, study on a weekend, 5 weeks in a row with hours logged.
- Hours: 1 / 10 / 50 / 100 / 250 / 500 hours, 4 hours in one day, 20 hours in one week.
- Mastery: 5 / 25 / 100 topics green, clear a whole term, clear a whole subject, every tracked topic green, zero red topics left.
- Exams & papers: first paper, 5 / 10 / 25 papers, 90%+ on 1 / 5 / 20 attempts, average above 80%, first AI marking, complete an upcoming exam, complete 5 exams.
- Setup & habits: add all subjects, add a notes doc, add a resource link, write today's goals 5 days running, reach level 5 / 10 / 25 / 50.

**Achievements page**

- Category tabs plus an "unlocked / locked / all" filter.
- Rarity styling (common, rare, epic, legendary) on the card border and glow, and a summary header with total unlocked, completion %, and rewards earned.
- Cards keep the current progress bar; newly unlocked ones animate in with a toast.

New metrics needed (derived from existing data, no new tables): best single-day hours, best week hours, per-term and per-subject completion, red-topic count, average score, exams completed, subjects with notes/links, goal-writing days.

## 2. Links opening in a new tab

Every external link (past papers, assessment notifications, Studoco / Atomi / THSC / NESA / Oakhill / Leibniz, Memorise By Heart, notes docs) already uses `target="_blank"`. What I'll do:

- Add `rel="noopener noreferrer"` everywhere it's missing so new tabs are safe and don't leak the referrer.
- Auto-prefix `https://` when a pasted link has no scheme — the likely reason a link ever fails to open.
- Make the whole quick-link tile clickable rather than just the icon, with a small "opens in new tab" arrow.

## 3. Things I think are missing (my picks, in order)

1. **Pomodoro / focus timer** that auto-logs the session into the study calendar and awards XP — removes the manual hour entry step.
2. **Marks and assessment weighting tracker** — enter each assessment mark and weight per subject, see running subject rank estimate and what you need on remaining tasks.
3. **Spaced-repetition flashcards** generated by Atlas from your notes, with a daily review queue.
4. **Weekly review** — a Sunday summary card: hours vs goal, topics turned green, weakest subject, and next week's suggested focus.
5. **Leaderboard-style personal bests** — longest streak, best week, most productive subject.

Tell me which of these to build next; default is the focus timer and the marks tracker.
